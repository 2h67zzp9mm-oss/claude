# Art prompts for the prologue game

These prompts ask for new paintings in the style of the family's Blacksteel artwork.
Paste one into the image tool you used for the crew pictures, then send me the results.
I'll put each picture in the game and line up the tappable objects with it.

## Style line (add this to every prompt)

> dark painterly fantasy illustration, highly detailed, black and brass steampunk pirate aesthetic, gold filigree, deep purple accents, dramatic rim lighting, rich shadows, cinematic, wide 5:3 frame

## Sizes

- **Backgrounds:** wide, 5:3 (for example 1600×960). No people in them; the characters are added by the game.
- **Characters:** full body, head to boots, on a plain flat background (solid gray or green is fine). I cut them out.

## Canon notes for the artist

- In the prologue, **Nightforge is an ordinary, dull dark-gray saber**. No glow, no purple, no cracks, until the "Not them" moment.
- Sean doesn't carry his **hammer** in the prologue. Mercer gives him a short pistol on the beach.
- The Bellgrave bells are carved or painted **without clappers**.
- Lily's doll: a faded straw-yellow dress, one **brown** button eye and one **blue**, three rows of black thread on the left arm.
- The **Record-Stone**'s writing must not be readable.

## Chapter One backgrounds

1. **Sean's cabin (the main playable room).** Cramped stern cabin of a small, patched-up frigate at night. A wooden door on the left with iron strap hinges. A coat hook with a long dark coat. A plain dark-gray saber in its scabbard on two wall pegs. A wooden table in the middle with a brass long-range communicator (small glowing screen, antenna), a dinner plate of fish sitting on a sea chart, loose receipts, and a book of tide tables. A brass porthole on the right showing a moonlit sea. A bunk with a shelf above it (books, a green bottle, a brass compass). A tin bucket catching a drip from a leak. A brass lantern hanging from the ceiling beam. A clear wooden floor across the front for walking. Warm lantern light, cold moonlight from the porthole.
2. **Shannon's archive at night.** Tall shelves of leather-bound books and scroll cubbies, a ladder, an arched window with rain, a desk lit by a brass oil lamp. On the desk: a burned map fragment (edges charred), stacked books, an open ledger, an inkwell and quill.
3. **Lily's room, morning.** A small, crooked two-room island house. Sunlight in golden bars through patched green shutters. Tiny bells painted along the ceiling beam, none with clappers. A chipped blue washbasin. A patchwork quilt, with a cloth doll on it (see the doll note above).
4. **Bellgrave village at dawn.** A bright fishing village of colorful houses built in widening rings around an old white hilltop shrine. Purple flowers in window boxes. Black basalt cliffs, crooked pines, a small harbor with fishing boats. The sea turning gold beyond the reef.
5. **The porch.** Porch steps of a small house overlooking the sea. A plate of bread with bright red jam, and Lily's doll propped beside it.
6. **Ships in the fog.** From the island at sunrise: a bank of fog far offshore with the dark shapes of warships hidden inside it. Seabirds rising off the golden water.
7. **Bellgrave burning.** Night. An island of black cliffs and crooked pines rising from fog, its harbor glowing orange and burning under a sky without stars. Seven gray warships in a steel crescent around the harbor, with their cannons aimed inland.

## Characters

1. **Sean, standing, facing left.** Full body on a plain background. Dark coat, a plain dark-gray saber at his hip, no hammer, no glow.
2. **Sean, standing, facing right.** Same outfit.
3. **Sean, walking:** 4 to 8 poses mid-stride, facing right. Same outfit, same size in every picture.
4. **Shannon:** head and shoulders, for her portrait.
5. **Deke:** head and shoulders (Fleet lieutenant: goggles with one lens missing, a long rifle on his back, a torn uniform).
6. **Lily:** head and shoulders, a young island girl holding her doll.
