# World Overview

## Towns

- **Little Town** is the original default town.
- **Copper & Clover** is the garden village cared for by Moss, Pip, and Nell.
- **Patchwork Commons** is the makers' town with Juno, Tavi, and Dottie.
- **Riverlight** is the waterside town with Wren, Lumi, and Otis.

## Caretaker priorities

1. Keep residents' awareness local and grounded.
2. Preserve relationships, memories, and save compatibility across travel.
3. Turn repeated observations into clear proposals.
4. Coordinate safe playful events with Mr. E.
5. Keep every game change testable and reversible.
