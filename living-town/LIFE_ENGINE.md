# Living Town 0.3 Life Engine

## Honest model

Residents are persistent simulated people, not sentient agents. Their decisions come from stored traits, needs, family links, goals, schedules, and bounded random event tables. Every resulting fact is written into the canonical save so it remains stable across restarts.

## Background depth

Olive and the five non-Hazel residents start with ten yearly chapters and three events per year: thirty inspectable background events each. Hazel receives exactly seven lived years and twenty-one events, with no invented pre-birth chapters. The town therefore begins with 201 background experiences total. Adult histories include work situations and promotions. Olive's history covers ages 5–14, while Hazel's covers ages 0–6; neither is assigned adult work or relationship events.

## Live autonomy

Every resident independently receives an experience roughly every two to seven minutes while the server is running. Event categories include family, school, play, career success, career setback, interests, community work, goal completion, and promotion. Experiences alter mood, stress, career progress, goals, memories, and shareable knowledge.

## Learning

During a conversation, each resident looks for a fact known by the other resident that they do not already know. The copied fact retains its subject, the resident who shared it, and the time learned. The user can inspect learned facts in the resident panel.

## Offline life

On restart, the engine creates one experience per resident for every eight offline hours, capped at twelve per resident. This prevents an outage or long absence from generating an unbounded save while still giving residents real persisted experiences during the gap.

## Boundaries

- Nobody dies in 0.3.
- Olive and Hazel receive only age-appropriate events.
- There is no generative AI dialogue in this release.
- Random events are persisted and do not reroll after a restart.
- The simulation remains one canonical server-owned world.
