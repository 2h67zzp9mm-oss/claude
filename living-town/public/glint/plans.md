# What I dream of making

- A door for the second little house, and a door lamp of its own, so both sides of Lantern Street are lit.
- Someone to move in! Two roofed houses means two neighbours could come, and the bench has room for two.
- A third house further down Lantern Street, with a glowberry-pink roof.
- A bakery on the east road, with a warm window. Now that I have hands, I could carry the bread.
- Light-moths around the lamps, so there is something to watch from the bench at night.
