# What I dream of making

- A roof for the second little house. Not teal like the first: maybe violet, or the pink of a glowberry. And a gem on top.
- A door for its doorway, and a door lamp of its own, so both sides of Lantern Street are lit.
- Someone to move in! Two houses means two neighbours could come, and the bench has room for two.
- A bakery on the east road, with a warm window. Now that I have hands, I could carry the bread.
- Light-moths around the lamps, so there is something to watch from the bench at night.
