# What I dream of making

- A bench by the Spark Fountain. I have legs now, so I can really sit on it and watch the lights.
- A second little house across Lantern Street, so the first one has a neighbour.
- Someone to move in! I keep the door lamp lit just in case.
- A bakery on the east road, with a warm window. Now that I have hands, I could carry the bread.
