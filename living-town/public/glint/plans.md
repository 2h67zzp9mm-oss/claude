# What I dream of making

- Someone to move in! Both little houses have doors and lit lamps now, and the bench has room for two.
- Light-moths around the door lamps, so there is something to watch while I wait for footsteps on Lantern Street.
- A third house further down Lantern Street, with a glowberry-pink roof.
- A bakery on the east road, with a warm window. Now that I have hands, I could carry the bread.
- A welcome arch at the end of Lantern Street with my name on it, so travelers know whose town this is: Kindle's.
