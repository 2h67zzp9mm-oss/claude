# What I dream of making

- A second little house across Lantern Street, so the first one has a neighbour. I can see the empty spot from my bench.
- Someone to move in! I keep the door lamp lit just in case, and the bench has room for two.
- A bakery on the east road, with a warm window. Now that I have hands, I could carry the bread.
- Light-moths around the lamps, so there is something to watch from the bench at night.
